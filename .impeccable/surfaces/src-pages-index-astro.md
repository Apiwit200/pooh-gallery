# Surface: the exhibition (homepage, collections, deep sky)

Scope: whole site. Visitor mode: Experience. The photograph leads from the first viewport.
Audience: visitors browsing Apiwit Nathong's photographs, often on a phone at night.
Constraints: PRODUCT.md. Every astrophotograph reachable. People-centric folders held until approved.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The archive is one descent through light. Photographs are ordered by the exposure value their own EXIF records, brightest noon first, deepest night sky last. Refuses the category default: a black page with a serif name over a hero and a masonry grid.

OWN-WORLD: The ground is a single committed field whose colour is a function of depth: daylight film-white, sea teal, aquarium blue, ink, then black with stars. A hairline light-meter rail on the right edge carries the whole EV scale in ghost ticks; only the current reading lights, in mono numerals. Type is a narrow grotesk for titles at extreme scale and a mono for readings. No cards, no rounded boxes, no glass.

STORY: The visitor falls from bright everyday scenes (roads, dogs, sea crossings) through dusk, aquarium and concert light into the deep sky, understands that the light itself is the subject, and can open any chapter or the full archive.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The brightest photograph of the archive full-bleed at 100svh. The title sits in the sky region at display scale, the credit small beneath. The EV rail is lit at its top reading. Minimal nav at top: index, deep sky, about. A quiet "descend" cue at the bottom.

FORM: The Descent (aquarium tunnel / light falloff), position 7 on the ordered list, fused with the mesophotic-dive challenger. Seed key 9ee2911b.

Raises: ghost-tick EV scale (seven-segment); total ground commitment (riso system); one vertical axis (glaze shelf); type and photo on separate scroll layers (riso zine); image front, facts on the flip (seed rack).

Signature interaction: the depth-driven ground and the EV rail, scrubbed by scroll, with the astro chapter ending in a restrained starfield.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
