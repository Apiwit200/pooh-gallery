# Surface: the exhibition (homepage, index, collections, archive, deep sky)

Scope: whole site redesign. Visitor mode: Experience. Replaces "The Descent" world; EV ordering dropped at the user's request.
Audience: visitors browsing Apiwit Nathong's photographs, often on a phone.
Constraints: PRODUCT.md. Every astrophotograph reachable. Held photos stay unpublished.
References pinned by the user: brik.space, thedesignsociety.fr, plnty.app. Techniques adapted, no code, fonts, marks or assets copied.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The archive is a late-night broadcast from a surreal set: an ultramarine CRT that plays photographs like channels. Refuses the category default of a white minimal gallery with a serif name over a grid.

OWN-WORLD: Ultramarine #350AFF fields, acid lime #BCF015 test-card tiles, cool off-white #EBEDF5 paper, near-black #03000F for the deep sky. Display in Jacquard 24 pixel blackletter, interface in Geist Pixel, body in Geist. A CRT layer (scanlines, glare, vignette, grain, RGB fringe) covers the whole screen and lifts from any photograph under the cursor. Authored pixel ornaments (an aperture sun). Square corners except the floating pill nav; no gradients as decoration; no glass.

STORY: The visitor tunes in, sees photographs thrown onto the screen as they move, reads a short transmission, flips through channels (collections) in a big index, drifts into the deep sky, and can open any channel or the whole archive.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full ultramarine screen. Centered pixel aperture ornament, then the title in Jacquard 24 at display scale with two real photographs set inline between words, a one-line credit in Geist Pixel, lime chip tags naming collections. Moving the pointer throws tilted real photographs behind the type (pool of 10). Floating pill nav top centre.

FORM: Pinned by the user (no roll), in their words: "Redesign time, use rea to reverse engineer BRIK.SPACE https://brik.space/Home and other websites attached to copy their designs ... I want something retro, surreal and abstract" (refs thedesignsociety.fr, plnty.app), later "when user hover their mouse over the channel name, I want the right side to be scrollitelling like this: https://tlb.betteroff.studio/surf/". Broadcast set fused from The Design Society (CRT, pixel ornaments, scramble type, gathering words, pixel transitions), Brik (inline images in headlines, colour chips, highlighted list index with swapping image), Plnty (cursor photo trail, tossed tilted cards), betteroff TLB surf (side-on photo corridor that rides the scroll in the channel guide).

Signature interaction: the CRT that clears exactly where you look (hovered photo) plus the pointer-thrown photo trail in the opening.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
